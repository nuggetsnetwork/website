Delhi, India

Built: before 2026-10-29, in an earlier session. Range: 736 to 1947 (rail 700 to 1950). Opens at: 1565. Chapter 1. Data: src/data/delhi.ts, src/data/delhi-realms.ts.

Sources

None were recorded. The earlier sessions that built this chapter left no web fetches or searches in their transcripts, so the houses, reigns, monuments, coordinates, realm rings and origin stories were written from the model's own knowledge. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons with credits in src/data/images.json.

Not verified

Everything. Specifically worth checking first:

  • The house boundaries and the choice of 736 (start of the Tomar house) and 1947.
  • Reign years of all 35 rulers.
  • Each of the 47 monuments: year, patron, coordinates, blurb.
  • Realm rings, region points and origins, in delhi-realms.ts.
  • A pass against a dynasty list such as the Wikipedia pages for the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughal Empire and the Tomar and Chauhan dynasties would be a sensible way to verify.

Exclusions

I did not record these when the chapter was built. This is what the data shows, not a record of intent.

  • Before 736: the earlier Indraprastha and Mauryan-era settlements, and anything before the Tomar house.
  • The rail runs 700 to 1950; the 36 and 3 years of padding are not history.
  • Only 10 of the roughly 20 Mughal emperors are listed, which looks like curation for the court arc. The Mughal house is shown with 10 rulers.
  • No separate entries for the Maratha hold on Delhi (about 1771 to 1803), Nader Shah's sack (1739) or the Hemu interlude (1556).

Judgement calls

These are visible in the data; I did not record them when the chapter was built.

  • Houses overlap: Sur (1540–1555) sits inside Mughal (1526–1857), and British (1803–1947) starts while the Mughals are still the nominal rulers (until 1857).
  • Gaps: Chauhan ends 1192 and Mamluk starts 1206 (Ghurid rule of the city, 1192–1206, has no house of its own).
  • This chapter predates the rule that the next house starts the year after the last ends, so some hand-offs share a year.
  • It predates the region-label check, so several region points fall outside their ring. check-city.cjs reports these as warnings for Delhi and as errors for every later city.
